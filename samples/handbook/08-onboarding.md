# Onboarding

## First week

On day one you receive your laptop, accounts and a buddy from your team. Complete the security
awareness training and sign the code of conduct within your first 5 days.

## 30-60-90 day plan

Your manager agrees a 30-60-90 day plan with you in the first week. A formal check-in happens
at the end of your probation period, which is 90 days.

## Payroll

Employees are paid monthly on the last working day of the month. Submit your bank details in
the HR portal before the 20th of your first month to be paid that month.
