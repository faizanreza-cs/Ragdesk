# Time Off Policy

Northwind Robotics is a fictional company. This handbook is sample data for ragdesk.

## Paid time off (PTO)

Full-time employees accrue 20 days of paid time off per calendar year, credited at 1.67 days
per month. Part-time employees accrue PTO pro rata to their contracted hours.

Up to 5 unused PTO days carry over into the next year. Carried-over days expire on March 31.
PTO is not paid out on resignation except where local law requires it.

## Requesting time off

Submit requests in the HR portal at least 10 working days in advance for absences longer than
three days. Your manager approves or declines within 2 working days.

## Public holidays

The company observes 11 public holidays a year. The list for each office is published in the
HR portal every December.

## Sick leave

Sick leave is separate from PTO: employees get 10 paid sick days per year. A doctor's note is
required for absences longer than 3 consecutive days.
