# Travel and Expenses

## Submitting expenses

Submit expense claims in Expensify within 30 days of the purchase. Attach an itemised receipt
for every item over $25. Claims are reimbursed in the next payroll run after approval.

## Approval limits

Managers approve claims up to $2,000. Claims above $2,000 also need approval from the
department's finance partner.

## Travel

Book flights and hotels through the company travel portal. Economy class is standard for
flights under 6 hours; premium economy is allowed for longer flights. The nightly hotel cap is
$250 in most cities and $350 in New York, London, San Francisco and Tokyo.

## Meals

The daily meal allowance while travelling is $75. Alcohol is not reimbursable except at
approved client entertainment events.

## Home office

New employees receive a one-time $500 home office stipend, claimable in their first 90 days.
