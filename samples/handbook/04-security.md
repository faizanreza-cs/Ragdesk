# Information Security

## Passwords and MFA

Use the company password manager for all work accounts. Multi-factor authentication is
mandatory for email, the HR portal, GitHub and the VPN. Hardware security keys are issued to
engineers and finance staff.

## Devices

Company laptops must have full-disk encryption and automatic screen lock after 5 minutes.
Personal devices may access email only through the managed mobile app.

## Reporting incidents

Report a lost or stolen device, or a suspected phishing email, to security@northwind.example
or in the #security-help Slack channel within 1 hour of noticing it. Do not try to
investigate phishing links yourself.

## Data classification

Data is classified as Public, Internal, Confidential or Restricted. Customer data is
Restricted and must never be pasted into unapproved AI tools or personal storage.
