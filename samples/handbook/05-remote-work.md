# Remote and Hybrid Work

## Core hours

Everyone is available for meetings during core hours, 10:00 to 15:00 in their local time zone.

## Hybrid schedule

Office-based teams come in on Tuesdays and Thursdays. Fully remote roles are marked as such in
the employment contract.

## Working from another country

You may work from another country for up to 30 days per calendar year. Longer stays need
approval from HR and Legal at least 4 weeks in advance because of tax and visa rules.

## Equipment

The company ships a laptop, monitor, keyboard and headset to remote employees. Return all
equipment within 14 days of leaving the company.
