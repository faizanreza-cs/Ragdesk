# IT Support

## Getting help

Open a ticket at help.northwind.example or message #it-help in Slack. Response targets are
4 business hours for standard requests and 30 minutes for P1 incidents that block work.

## Software requests

Request new software through the IT portal. Security reviews new SaaS tools within 5 business
days. Tools that process customer data also need a data protection review.

## VPN

Use the company VPN when accessing internal systems from public Wi-Fi. The VPN client is
pre-installed on company laptops.
