# Benefits

## Health insurance

Health, dental and vision insurance start on your first day. The company pays 90% of the
premium for employees and 75% for dependants.

## Retirement

The company matches 401(k) contributions dollar for dollar up to 5% of base salary. Matching
contributions vest immediately.

## Learning budget

Each employee has an annual learning budget of $1,500 for courses, books and conferences.
Unused budget does not roll over.

## Wellness

A monthly wellness stipend of $60 covers gym memberships, fitness classes or mental health
apps.
