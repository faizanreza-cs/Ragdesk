# Parental Leave

## Primary caregivers

Primary caregivers receive 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave, which can start up to two
weeks before the expected birth or adoption date.

## Secondary caregivers

Secondary caregivers receive 6 weeks of fully paid leave, to be taken within 12 months of the
birth or adoption.

## Eligibility

Parental leave is available after 90 days of continuous employment. Notify HR at least 8 weeks
before the planned start date.

## Returning to work

Returning parents may work a reduced 80% schedule at full pay for the first 4 weeks back.
